How the Light Shower implementation will work

  1. Sign up or pay

    GHL saves the person's details, chosen class, and purchase.

  2. Prepare the class list

    GHL holds the list of people who should receive that day's Zoom link.

  3. Create Zoom

    Pabbly creates the meeting about 50 minutes before class and sends the fresh link to GHL.

  4. Check attendance

    After class, Zoom reports who joined and how long they stayed.

  5. Save the recording

    The system checks that a private copy is safe in Google Drive.

  6. Update each person

    GHL records attendance, updates access or credits, and sends the right follow-up.

Keep sign-ups and payments organized

Save each person once in GHL, record what they chose, and send them to the correct checkout and confirmation page.

Send the correct daily class link

Create a fresh Zoom link about 50 minutes before class, check it, send reminders, and alert staff if the link is missing.

Record attendance

Match each Zoom guest to the right GHL contact, combine repeat joins, prevent duplicate records, and flag names that cannot be matched safely.

Manage the $99 plan

Use one credit only after verified Light Shower or Midweek attendance. Remote Midweek has no surcharge; in person adds $15. A no-show keeps the credit, and the matching replay ends exactly 72 hours after class.

Manage the $199 Unlimited Month

Start 30 days of access when payment succeeds, include remote Midweek, require $15 for in-person Midweek, count verified eligible attendance toward the 11-class policy, and end access on time.

Save recordings and control replays

Copy recordings to a private Google Drive folder, check the copy, give access only to the right people, and remove the Zoom copy after 72 hours when safe.

Send mode-safe messages

Send confirmations, reminders, cancellations, waitlist, mode-change, replay, and recovery messages. Remote sends contain no clinic instructions; in-person sends contain no Zoom link.

Track private and $499 services

For $399, confirm the Winnie or Jay appointment before payment. For $499, track payment, intake, the combined session, and all three Light Treasures.

People remain in control. Staff still handle unclear attendance, identity problems, special requests, refunds, failed connections, and sensitive support.

The Light Shower recovery order

Keep Midweek stable, prove Light Shower state and customer journeys first, then connect the time-sensitive Zoom, attendance, and replay layers.

  1. 1Freeze Midweek

    Record live IDs, owners, and rollback steps. Keep the production slice unchanged and leave other MW families deferred.

  2. 2Audit TCLW

    Back up the live funnel and inventory pages, calendars, products, fields, workflows, publishers, routes, and current failures.

  3. 3Build the GHL base

    Create or verify the four-day calendars, `$99/$199` products, state fields, forms, mode routing, and stop rules.

  4. 4Prove core journeys

    Test free, `$99`, and `$199` purchase, access, booking, reminder-preference, expiry, refund, and support paths without live traffic.

  5. 5Connect fresh links

    Adapt the Midweek pattern to M/F and T/Th publishers, occurrence validation, staff preflight, and remote-only link release.

  6. 6Connect messages, analytics, and Zoom last

    Prove attendance, no-show, credit, recording, replay, rollback, and a single canary occurrence; then expand by wave.
