## 1. Executive Summary

**Heart Center LA** is a healing brand based in Pasadena, California, founded by Dr. Winnie Chan Wang and Dr. Jay Dubois, PhD. The brand operates on a single governing principle:

> **"We provide spaces that encourage healing to happen."**

Heart Center LA creates structured environments — live events, classes, breathwork sessions, and institutional programs — where people can see their patterns, name what's stuck, and do the work that changes it. The method is proprietary: the **ARC framework** (Aware → Release → Choose & Commit), applied through somatic, relational, and energetic modalities that the founders have practiced for over two decades.

The brand's philosophical foundation rests on **The Three Harmonies**:

1. **Self Harmony** — alignment within the body (jaw, gut, pelvis)
2. **Heaven + Earth + Human Harmony** — alignment with natural rhythms through sacred breathwork
3. **Harmony with Other Humans** — healing in community, releasing trauma collectively

Both founders received the **World Cultural Harmony Award** (May 21, 2026) from the World Cultural Harmony Council and the International Alliance of Civilization, in recognition of their contributions to fostering intercultural dialogue and building inclusive communities aligned with the UN "Culture of Peace" ideal.

**The business model has four revenue streams:**

| Stream | Type | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Conscious Connections | Live dating event (Thursday nights) | Front door — audience acquisition, content, social proof |
| Heart Center Connection Podcast | Weekly, free | Audience building, vocabulary training, SEO |
| Healing Classes | 4-tier paid class packages | **Primary revenue** — the core product the site sells |
| Institutional Facilitation | Jay & Winnie hired for corporate, hospital, and community events | B2B premium revenue |

The healing classes are the center of gravity. Everything else — the event, the podcast, the institutional credibility — funnels people toward paid class enrollment.

## 2. Brand Architecture & Philosophy

### The Motto

> **"We provide spaces that encourage healing to happen."**

This is the public-facing brand promise. Heart Center LA doesn't sell healing as a transaction. It creates containers — physical, energetic, relational — where healing becomes possible. The space does the work. The facilitators hold the space. The participant does their own healing inside it.

### The Method: ARC Framework

ARC is the diagnostic and transformational method that operates inside every Heart Center LA offering. It exists at three registers:

| Register | Vocabulary | Audience |
|---|---|---|
| **Public** | See → Name → Change | First encounters, marketing, events |
| **Book / Course** | Aware → Release → Choose & Commit | Students, class participants, readers |
| **Practitioner** | Excavate → Interpret → Restore | Clients in deep work, trained practitioners |

Every event follows an ARC arc. Every podcast episode walks a listener through ARC. Every class series moves participants through the full ARC sequence. The vocabulary compounds — by the time someone books a private session, they already speak the language.

**This is the moat.** Not "we have a framework," but "we have a vocabulary our audience has learned from us, and no one else can speak it credibly."

### The Philosophy: Three Harmonies

The Three Harmonies provide the worldview beneath the method. ARC is *how* healing happens. The Three Harmonies are *why* it matters.

The Three Harmonies introduce the founders in narrative sequence — Winnie alone, then Jay alone, then both together:

**1. Self Harmony (Winnie)**
The harmony between upper body (jaw), middle body (gut), and lower body (pelvis). This is the somatic foundation. Dr. Winnie Chan Wang developed the **Jaw-Gut-Pelvis method** to address this alignment clinically. When the body is in harmony with itself, healing has a foundation to build on.

*Maps to: Healing classes, private sessions*

**2. Heaven + Earth + Human Harmony (Jay)**
The vertical integration — connecting the individual to something larger than themselves. Dr. Jay Dubois, PhD brings this through his work in anthropology, relational coaching, and the ARC framework. This harmony is about aligning with Heaven's timing, Earth's conditions, and Human Harmony through sacred breathwork and the practice of connecting personal work with universal rhythms.

*Maps to: Daily breathwork, Heart Center LA classes, ARC coaching*

**3. Harmony with Other Humans (Both)**
Healing in relationship. Releasing trauma in community. This is why Winnie and Jay founded the **Compassionate Transformation Community** — a nonprofit focused on mental wellness, trauma release, and self-healing education. This harmony is also what Conscious Connections (the dating event) exists to create.

*Maps to: Conscious Connections event, Compassionate Transformation Community, institutional facilitation (hospitals, corporate)*

> **"Each person, every time they show up to a lightwall session, Heart Center Connection, retreat — they are not just doing it for themselves, they are doing it for world peace. Everything that they do for themselves they project to the world. Heal yourself, heal the world."** — Winnie Chan Wang

> **"If you want to heal the world, look inside your body."** — Winnie Chan Wang

## 4. Offer Stack & Revenue Model

### The Funnel Logic

```
STRANGER
  │
  ├── Discovers podcast (social clip, SEO, friend shares episode)
  │     └── Follows on social → sees event promo → attends Conscious Connections
  │
  ├── Sees Instagram/TikTok clip → follows → binge-listens podcast
  │     └── Attends event or explores classes directly
  │
  ├── Attends Conscious Connections event (word of mouth, Eventbrite, ad)
  │     └── Gets follow-up sequence → explores healing classes
  │
  └── Institutional contact (hospital, corporate) → books Jay & Winnie for facilitation
        └── Organization becomes referral source for individual classes

EVERY PATH LEADS TO THE HEALING CLASSES.
```

### Stream 1: Conscious Connections (Live Event)

**What it is:** A conscious speed dating experience for spiritually-open singles in their 20s and 30s.

**When:** Thursday nights

**Where:** Atelier by Artmingo, 3330 E Colorado Blvd, Pasadena, CA

**Format:**
1. **Arrival + Beverages** — people show up, settle in, social energy builds
2. **Communal Breathing Practice** — Winnie-style grounding breathwork shifts the room out of "just arrived" energy
3. **Guided Question Rounds** — prompt-driven conversations structured to rotate through people, escalating in depth across rounds
4. **Conscious Speed Dating** — structured connection with ARC-informed prompts

**The ARC arc in the room:**
- Opening (SEE): Breathwork and intention-setting surface what each person carries into connection
- Core (NAME): Prompted rounds make patterns visible through live interaction
- Close (CHANGE): Each person leaves with something named — a pattern seen, a connection felt, a next step clear

**Role in the business:** Front door. Generates three things the brand needs: testimonials, content (photos/video), and warm leads for healing classes. Revenue from ticket sales is secondary to what the event produces for the ecosystem.

**Ticket price range:** $45–65

**Capacity:** 20–30 per event

**Cadence:** Monthly minimum, bi-weekly if demand supports it

**Content capture:** Every event produces Instagram stories, short-form video clips, candid photos, and testimonial footage. This content feeds the podcast, social channels, and website.

### Stream 2: Heart Center Connection Podcast

**What it is:** A free weekly podcast where real listener questions are walked through the ARC framework on air.

**Format (advice column model):**
- Listeners submit letters describing their relational patterns
- Jay reads the letter (or paraphrases for anonymity)
- Names the pattern (AWARE) — "Here's what I see happening"
- Names what's stuck in the body (RELEASE) — "Here's what's underneath"
- Names the real choice (CHOOSE & COMMIT) — "Here's what actually changes this"
- Bridge CTA: "If you heard yourself in this, that's the pattern. Write to us." → letter submission form or Conscious Connections event

**Episode formats:**
| Format | Structure | Duration | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Diagnostic Round** | 3 listener letters, ~10 min each | 30–40 min | Social clips, SEO, shareability |
| **Deep Dive** | 1 letter, full ARC walkthrough | 45 min | Listener intimacy, depth |
| **On-Air Session** | Real client, real work (anonymized, consented) | 30 min | Highest differentiation, Esther Perel territory |

**Role in the business:** Free audience builder. Trains listeners in ARC vocabulary. Each episode is a public demonstration of the method — proof that it works before anyone pays. The most clippable format (each letter = 1 Reel) feeds social media discovery.

**Cadence:** Weekly (1 episode), with 2–3 social clips pulled per episode.

### Stream 3: Healing Classes (PRIMARY REVENUE)

**This is the core product. The website exists to sell these classes.**

The healing classes are structured in four tiers that meet people where they are — from light renewal to identity-level transformation:

| Tier | Price | What It Is | What Changes | Use When |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Refresh** | $35 | A light renewal. You're still fundamentally the same — just less tired. | Energy lifts, mental clarity improves, emotional load lightens | You're functional but fatigued |
| **Reset** | $84 | A conscious interruption of a pattern. You stop, clear, and re-orient. | Habits, focus, short-term behaviors, nervous system state | You're stuck in a loop and need a clean slate |
| **Restore** | $196 | Repairing something that has been depleted, damaged, or lost. | Capacity, baseline health, emotional or physical stability | Something essential has been worn down and needs rebuilding |
| **Revitalize** | $238/month | A full month of classes. Not just repair — renewal at a higher level than before. | Identity, energy baseline, purpose, direction | You are ready to become more than you were before |

**What doesn't change at lower tiers (and why higher tiers exist):**
- Refresh doesn't touch core patterns, structures, or identity
- Reset doesn't reach deep wounds or long-standing identity structures
- Restore addresses burnout, chronic stress, grief, long-term depletion
- Revitalize is where new vitality, creativity, meaning, and expansion emerge

**Examples people can relate to:**
- Refresh = a good night's sleep, a walk, a weekend off
- Reset = a digital detox, a short retreat, recommitting to boundaries
- Restore = burnout recovery, trauma-informed healing, deep rest over time
- Revitalize = life-altering retreats, major healing breakthroughs, post-crisis transformation

**The pricing psychology:** The ladder is designed so that someone can enter at $35 (one class, low commitment) and see enough to understand that deeper work requires deeper investment. The jump from $35 → $84 → $196 → $238/mo follows the pattern: relief → interruption → repair → renewal. Each tier answers a more serious version of the same question.

**What the site needs to communicate:**
1. Which tier is right for you (self-assessment language, not clinical diagnosis)
2. What actually happens in a session (demystify without deflating)
3. Who facilitates (Winnie's credentials are load-bearing here)
4. Social proof (testimonials, the UN award, partner logos)
5. Clear booking CTA at every tier

## 6. Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1–2)

**Objective:** Generate social proof and validate the class product.

1. **Run 3–5 people through healing classes for free or at deep discount.** Hand-pick from warmest contacts. The price: they do the work seriously and give an honest video testimonial at the end.
2. **Launch Conscious Connections Event #1.** Fill the room via personal networks, Instagram, and direct invites. Priority is proof of concept + testimonial harvest, not ticket revenue.
3. **Open the podcast letter submission form.** Seed the pipeline with 10+ letters from event attendees, personal network, and existing community before recording Episode 1 in the new format.
4. **Update heartcenter.la** with the 4-tier class structure, UN award, and any testimonials collected.
5. **Pause or minimize paid ad spend** until real testimonials exist on the site.

### Phase 2: Traction (Months 3–4)

**Objective:** Establish repeating cadence across all streams.

1. **Monthly Conscious Connections events** running consistently. Each event produces testimonials, content, and class leads.
2. **Weekly podcast episodes** publishing in the new advice-column format. 2–3 social clips per episode.
3. **Healing classes** enrolling organically from event follow-up sequences, podcast CTAs, and social content.
4. **5+ real video testimonials** on the website. Paid ad spend can resume once testimonials are live.
5. **First institutional inquiry** generated from updated website + existing partner relationships.

### Phase 3: Scale (Months 5–12)

**Objective:** Grow class enrollment, expand institutional work, build waitlists.

1. **Increase event frequency** to bi-weekly if demand supports it.
2. **Launch paid advertising** for healing classes, backed by real testimonials and the UN award.
3. **Pitch institutional facilitation** to 3–5 new organizations per quarter using existing partner logos and the World Cultural Harmony Award as social proof.
4. **Evaluate retreat offering** — co-led by Jay and Winnie, premium price point, annual or biannual.
5. **Grow podcast** to the point where it generates inbound class inquiries without paid promotion.

## 10. Risks & Dependencies

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **No testimonials** | Can't convert strangers. Ad spend is wasted. | Phase 1 is entirely focused on generating social proof before scaling. Run free/discounted sessions specifically for testimonial collection. |
| **Jay burns out** | Podcast stops, events stop, public voice goes quiet. The brand depends heavily on his presence. | Build a content buffer (batch record podcast episodes). Design events that can run with a substitute facilitator. Develop Winnie's public presence as a second voice. |
| **Winnie's capacity** | She leads all classes, daily breathwork, and private sessions. If she's overextended, quality drops. | Monitor class load. Hire assistant facilitators as class enrollment grows. Protect the daily practice as non-negotiable. |
| **Events don't fill** | No testimonials, no content, no warm leads. The funnel stalls. | First 2–3 events should be filled via personal network, not cold advertising. Lower ticket price or invite-only if needed to guarantee a full room. |
| **Podcast doesn't convert** | Listenership grows but nobody books classes. | Every episode must end with a clear CTA. Track which episodes generate class inquiries. Adjust format based on data. |
| **Institutional sales cycle is long** | Hospitals and corporations don't move fast. Revenue is unpredictable. | Don't depend on institutional revenue for operating costs. Treat it as upside. Use existing partner relationships as warm introductions. |
| **GHL technical issues** | Booking flow breaks, payments don't process, emails don't send. | Dedicate time to fully wire GHL before Phase 2. Test every flow. Have a manual backup (DM/email booking) for the first month. |
| **Competitive landscape** | Other conscious dating events or healing brands in LA. | Heart Center LA's moat is the ARC framework + Winnie's clinical credentials + the UN award. No competitor in LA has this combination. Protect and deepen the proprietary vocabulary. |
