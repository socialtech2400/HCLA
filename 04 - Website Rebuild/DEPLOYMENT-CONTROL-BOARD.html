What changes

What this upgrade will automate

It turns the current meeting-link handoff into a tracked customer system, while keeping uncertain cases visible to staff.

Current system

  • Pabbly creates each weekday Zoom meeting shortly before class.
  • It cleans the join link and sends that link to a GHL inbound webhook.
  • The receiving GHL actions still need inspection before the link update is treated as verified.
  • The current publisher does not collect attendance or archive recordings.
  • Most access, credit, follow-up, and exception handling still depends on manual work.

After the upgrade

  • GHL owns each contact's purchase, access, credits, benefits, messages, and review state.
  • Zoom supplies meeting and participant evidence; Pabbly transports and normalizes it.
  • Drive holds verified recording copies before Zoom cleanup occurs.
  • Automations act on verified events and stop for staff review when records disagree.

How one live class will move through the upgraded system

  1. Register or purchase

    GHL records the contact, selected class, consent, source, and paid offer.

  2. Build the roster

    The contact ID and class occurrence are queued before the Zoom meeting exists.

  3. Create Zoom at T-50

    Pabbly keeps the current under-one-hour rule and publishes the fresh link.

  4. Collect evidence

    Zoom returns the occurrence, participant joins and leaves, and recording event.

  5. Normalize and verify

    Pabbly combines repeat joins; the archive path verifies the private Drive copy.

  6. Update GHL safely

    GHL logs attendance, adjusts access, sends follow-up, or creates a staff task.

Capture registrations and route payments

Create or update one GHL contact, preserve consent and attribution, record the selected class or appointment, and route each buyer to the correct checkout and confirmation path.

Deliver the correct daily class link

Preserve the intentional T-50 Zoom creation window, verify the fresh meeting data, send reminders only after preflight, and alert staff when the link is missing or stale.

Log and reconcile attendance

Match the GHL contact to the Zoom occurrence, combine repeat joins, prevent duplicate attendance, record verified attendance or no-show, and send uncertain identities to manual review.

Manage $99 credits and three-day access

Deduct one of three credits only after verified attendance, leave credits unchanged for no-shows, grant the matching replay, and revoke it exactly 72 hours after the scheduled class ends.

Manage the $199 Unlimited Month

Start exactly 30 consecutive days at payment, enroll daily reminders, track the 11 verified attendances used by the satisfaction policy, preserve the no-expiration 15-minute benefit, and end live access automatically.

Archive recordings and control replay

Copy completed Zoom recordings to restricted Drive, verify the copy, grant only the correct replay window, and delete the Zoom copy after 72 hours only when the archive is confirmed.

Run follow-up, benefits, refunds, and support

Send confirmations and reminders, stop incompatible nurture, track benefit use and policy eligibility, separate payment failures, and create staff tasks when a decision is needed.

Protect private and premium fulfillment

For $399, require a confirmed Winnie or Jay appointment before payment and enforce the 24-hour reschedule rule. For $499, track payment, intake, the combined session, and all three Light Treasures.

People remain in control. Staff still resolve ambiguous attendance, identity mismatches, exceptions, refunds, in-person requests, failed integrations, and sensitive support cases.
