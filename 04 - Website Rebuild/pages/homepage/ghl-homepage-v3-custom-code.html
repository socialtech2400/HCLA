The method

See the pattern. Name what is held. Change what comes next.

ARC is the Heart Center LA framework for moving from awareness into embodied choice.

In public language, it is simple: See, Name, Change. In class, students move through Aware, Release, Choose and Commit. In deeper practice, the same arc becomes Excavate, Interpret, Restore.

Begin with a class
Aware

Notice the pattern

Breathwork, reflection, and guided attention make the loop visible enough to work with.

Release

Let the body respond

Somatic and energetic practices help discharge what has been held in the nervous system.

Choose

Commit to the next step

Each class closes with a real choice that can be practiced outside the room.

Why it works

The Three Harmonies

ARC is how the practice moves. The Three Harmonies are the worldview beneath it: the body, the natural rhythm, and the human relationship.

Self Harmony

Alignment within the body, including the jaw, gut, and pelvis. This is the somatic foundation of the work Dr. Winnie Chan Wang brings to Heart Center LA.

Heaven + Earth + Human Harmony

Daily sacred breathwork and practice that reconnect participants with timing, conditions, and a larger field of support.

Harmony with Other Humans

Healing in relationship, naming patterns in community, and practicing a different way of being with one another.
