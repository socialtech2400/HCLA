Executive Summary

Heart Center LA is a healing brand based in Pasadena, California, founded by Dr. Winnie Chan Wang and Dr. Jay Dubois, PhD. The brand creates structured environments — live events, classes, breathwork sessions, and institutional programs — where people can see their patterns, name what's stuck, and do the work that changes it.

The method is proprietary: the ARC framework (Aware → Release → Choose & Commit), applied through somatic, relational, and energetic modalities that the founders have practiced for over two decades. The philosophical foundation rests on The Three Harmonies — self harmony, heaven-earth-human harmony, and harmony with other humans.

Both founders received the World Cultural Harmony Award (May 21, 2026) from the World Cultural Harmony Council and the International Alliance of Civilization, in recognition of their contributions to fostering intercultural dialogue and building inclusive communities aligned with the UN "Culture of Peace" ideal.

Brand Architecture & Philosophy

The Method: ARC Framework

ARC is the diagnostic and transformational method inside every Heart Center LA offering. It exists at three registers, deepening as the audience deepens their engagement with the brand.

RegisterVocabularyAudience
PublicSee → Name → ChangeFirst encounters, marketing, events
Book / CourseAware → Release → Choose & CommitStudents, class participants, readers
PractitionerExcavate → Interpret → RestoreClients in deep work, trained practitioners
This is the moat. Not "we have a framework," but "we have a vocabulary our audience has learned from us, and no one else can speak it credibly."

The Philosophy: Three Harmonies

ARC is how healing happens. The Three Harmonies are why it matters.

First Harmony

Self Harmony

The harmony between upper body (jaw), middle body (gut), and lower body (pelvis). Dr. Winnie Chan Wang developed the Jaw-Gut-Pelvis method to address this alignment clinically. When the body is in harmony with itself, healing has a foundation.

Maps to → Healing classes, private sessions
Second Harmony

Heaven + Earth + Human Harmony

Following Heaven's timing, Earth's conditions, and Human Harmony. Achieved through daily sacred breathwork — the 7am practice Winnie leads through Heart Center LA. This connects the individual to something larger than themselves.

Maps to → Daily breathwork, Heart Center LA classes
Third Harmony

Harmony with Other Humans

Healing in relationship. Releasing trauma in community. Winnie and Jay founded the Compassionate Transformation Community for mental wellness, trauma release, and self-healing education. This is also what Conscious Connections exists to create.

Maps to → Events, community, institutional facilitation

Offer Stack & Revenue Model

Funnel Logic

Every path leads to the healing classes.

STRANGER | +-- Discovers podcast (social clip, SEO, friend shares episode) | +-- Follows on social -> sees event promo -> attends Conscious Connections | +-- Sees Instagram/TikTok clip -> follows -> binge-listens podcast | +-- Attends event or explores classes directly | +-- Attends Conscious Connections (word of mouth, Eventbrite, ad) | +-- Gets follow-up sequence -> explores healing classes | +-- Institutional contact (hospital, corporate) -> books Jay & Winnie +-- Organization becomes referral source for individual classes EVERY PATH LEADS TO THE HEALING CLASSES.

Stream 1: Conscious Connections (Live Event)

A conscious speed dating experience for spiritually-open singles in their 20s and 30s. Thursday nights at Atelier by Artmingo, Pasadena.

Format

  1. Arrival + Beverages
  2. Communal Breathing Practice
  3. Guided Question Rounds (structured rotation, escalating depth)
  4. Conscious Speed Dating

The ARC Arc in the Room

  • SEE: Breathwork surfaces what each person carries into connection
  • NAME: Prompted rounds make patterns visible through live interaction
  • CHANGE: Each person leaves with something named — a pattern seen, a connection felt

Business Role

Front door. Generates testimonials, content (photos/video), and warm leads for healing classes. Revenue from tickets ($45–65) is secondary to what the event produces for the ecosystem.

Capacity: 20–30 per event
Cadence: Monthly min, bi-weekly if demand supports

Stream 2: Heart Center Connection Podcast

A free weekly podcast where real listener questions are walked through the ARC framework on air. The advice column model.

FormatStructureDurationBest For
Diagnostic Round3 listener letters, ~10 min each30–40 minSocial clips, SEO, shareability
Deep Dive1 letter, full ARC walkthrough45 minListener intimacy, depth
On-Air SessionReal client, real work (anonymized)30 minHighest differentiation

Every episode ends with a clear CTA: "If you heard yourself in this, that's the pattern." → letter submission form, event, or class booking.

Go-to-Market Strategy

Foundation

Generate social proof and validate the class product.
  1. Run 3–5 people through healing classes for free or at deep discount. Collect video testimonials.
  2. Launch Conscious Connections Event #1. Fill via personal networks. Priority: proof of concept + testimonial harvest.
  3. Open podcast letter submission form. Seed with 10+ letters before recording Episode 1.
  4. Update heartcenter.la with 4-tier class structure, UN award, and any testimonials collected.
  5. Pause or minimize paid ad spend until real testimonials exist on site.

Traction

Establish repeating cadence across all streams.
  1. Monthly Conscious Connections events running consistently.
  2. Weekly podcast episodes in advice-column format. 2–3 social clips per episode.
  3. Healing classes enrolling organically from event follow-ups, podcast CTAs, social.
  4. 5+ real video testimonials on the website. Resume paid ads.
  5. First institutional inquiry generated from updated website + existing relationships.

Scale

Grow class enrollment, expand institutional work, build waitlists.
  1. Increase event frequency to bi-weekly if demand supports it.
  2. Launch paid advertising for healing classes backed by real testimonials + UN award.
  3. Pitch institutional facilitation to 3–5 new organizations per quarter.
  4. Evaluate retreat offering — co-led, premium price point, annual or biannual.
  5. Grow podcast to generate inbound class inquiries without paid promotion.
